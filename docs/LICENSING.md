# Licence and permitted use

The project's original material uses the [ST-DFSO Non-Commercial Academic Research Licence](../LICENSE).
It permits non-commercial academic research, reproduction, scholarly evaluation and teaching, including publication of findings. It does not permit commercial products, paid services or commercial operational use. Modifications must preserve attribution and the use restrictions when shared. Commercial permission requires a separate written agreement with the relevant rights holders.

This is source-available research software. A prohibition on commercial use is not compatible with the [Open Source Initiative's definition](https://opensource.org/osd), which permits use in any field, including business. There is no single licence required for all academic papers.

The following published research repositories illustrate different approaches; their licences have not been copied into ST-DFSO or treated as a grant over this project:

| Research code | Approach | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3D Gaussian Splatting | Project-specific research and non-commercial licence | [Official licence](https://github.com/graphdeco-inria/gaussian-splatting/blob/main/LICENSE.md) |
| StyleGAN3 | NVIDIA source-code licence restricting use to non-commercial research or evaluation | [Official licence](https://github.com/NVlabs/stylegan3/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) |
| DUSt3R | Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 | [Official licence](https://github.com/naver/dust3r/blob/main/LICENSE) |

Creative Commons [recommends software-specific licences for software](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#can-i-apply-a-creative-commons-license-to-software). ST-DFSO therefore states its software permissions directly instead of applying a Creative Commons licence indiscriminately to code and third-party data.

The project licence covers only rights its contributors can grant. Dependencies and environmental data retain the permissions and attribution requirements listed in [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Their original permissions are not narrowed by ST-DFSO's non-commercial condition.
